Who is Sad Mazda Songs?

Discover the Quebec underground rap artist

Sad Mazda Songs (abbreviated SMS) is an underground rap music group from Quebec City, Canada. Formed in 2024, this Quebec rap group stands out with a unique and bold style that defies traditional rap conventions.

The trio composed of Marc, L-P and Sam creates music that blends absurd punchlines, basement humor, tech references and unexpected beats. Their unapologetically off-beat approach and overflowing confidence make Sad Mazda Songs a Quebec rap group you absolutely must discover.

Contrary to what the name might suggest, Sad Mazda Songs has no affiliation with the Mazda automobile company or its advertisements. The Group's History

Founded in 2024 in the dangerously quiet streets of Quebec City, Sad Mazda Songs was born from a simple but bold mission: to prove that you can make rap, be off-beat... and still sound better than your favorite artist.

The group quickly made a name for itself in the Quebec underground rap scene thanks to its unique approach to rap. Between Mazda rides with the volume cranked up, SMS drops bars that talk as much about algorithms as they do about feats... or feet pics at 40 bucks.

Their philosophy is simple: if the beat sounds crooked, it's not a mistake — the future just arrived too soon for you. This offbeat approach makes Sad Mazda Songs a Quebec rap group like nothing else in the music scene.

Musical Style

The music of Sad Mazda Songs is characterized by an explosive mix of Quebec underground rap, absurd punchlines and basement guy humor. The group creates a unique musical universe where tech references meet everyday absurdity.

Their musical style is distinguished by:

  • Unexpected beats — Original productions and unapologetically off-beat
  • Absurd punchlines — Unique lyrics blending humor and technique
  • Basement humor — An offbeat and unfiltered approach
  • Tech references — Lyrics that talk as much about algorithms as rap
  • Overflowing confidence — "Worthy of a 3G modem that still thinks it dominates the internet"

This combination makes Sad Mazda Songs an essential Quebec rap group in the underground scene, with a musical identity like no other.
